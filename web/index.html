Satellite land use and change detection in your browser

Two models from one project, quantised to INT8 and run with onnxruntime-web. Every image is processed on your device; nothing is uploaded.

A ResNet-18 trained on all 13 Sentinel-2 bands of EuroSAT, next to the same network trained on red, green and blue only. Pick a 64×64 test patch or upload a 13-band EuroSAT GeoTIFF.

A Siamese U-Net (shared ResNet-18 encoder, absolute feature difference at every scale) trained on LEVIR-CD, 0.5 m aerial tiles. It marks new or demolished buildings, not roads, fields or seasons. Pick a test pair or upload two images of the same place (resized to 256×256).

On the 5,400 EuroSAT test patches the all-13-band model scores 0.987 and the RGB model 0.983; the extra bands help most on crops and pasture. On the LEVIR-CD test split the change model reaches F1 0.89. EuroSAT's random split flatters both classifiers, and the change model has only seen 0.5 m aerial imagery of Texas.

Data: EuroSAT (Helber et al. 2019, MIT), Copernicus Sentinel-2 (ESA); LEVIR-CD (Chen & Shi 2020, academic use), example tiles from its test split. Code, training and evaluation: GitHub repo.
